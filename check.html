This may be an emergency — contact a veterinarian now

What you described can be a sign of a serious problem. Call your vet or the nearest emergency vet right away. Do not wait to see if it improves, and do not try home remedies.

What to do now:

  • Call ahead: Phone your vet or an emergency clinic so they can tell you what to do and prepare for your arrival.
  • Do not induce vomiting unless a veterinarian or poison control specialist tells you to.
  • Travel safely: Keep your pet calm and secure in a carrier or wrapped gently in a blanket.
