How can we help you and your furry friend today?

🛒 Ordering & Payments

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, and Apple Pay for a seamless checkout experience.
Can I modify or cancel my order?
We process orders quickly to ensure fast delivery! You can modify or cancel your order within 1 hour of placing it by contacting our support team. After that, the order will be shipped and you can utilize our easy return process.
Where is my order confirmation email?
Order confirmations are sent immediately after checkout. If you haven't received yours within 5 minutes, please check your spam/junk folder. Still not there? Contact us and we'll resend it.

📦 Shipping & Delivery

How much is shipping?
We offer FREE standard shipping on all orders over $49! For orders under $49, standard shipping is a flat rate of $5.99. Express options are also available at checkout.
How long will it take to get my order?
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days, while Express takes 2-3 business days.
Do you ship internationally?
Currently, we only ship within the United States (including APO/FPO addresses). We are working hard to bring My Wow Pet to international furry friends soon!

↩️ Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-Day Happiness Guarantee! Unopened items can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. For health and safety reasons, we cannot accept returns on opened food or treats.
How do I start a return?
Simply head to our Returns page or contact our support team with your order number. We'll provide you with a prepaid return label.
How long do refunds take?
Once we receive your return, we process it within 2 business days. It may take 5-7 business days for the refund to reflect on your original payment method depending on your bank.

👤 Account & Subscribe

How does Subscribe & Save work?
Never run out of your pet's favorites! Select "Subscribe & Save" on any product to get a 10% discount on every delivery. You can choose your delivery frequency (every 2, 4, or 6 weeks) and cancel or pause anytime from your account.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
Click "Login" at the top of the page, then click "Forgot Password?". Enter your email, and we'll send you a secure link to reset your password.
